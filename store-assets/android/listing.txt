APP NAME
APeX: Tennis Swing Analysis

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Film your tennis strokes. Get swing count, swing range and knee bend feedback.

FULL DESCRIPTION
APeX analyzes your tennis swing from a short phone video. Prop your phone up, record a few strokes, and APeX reconstructs your movement in 3D to count your swings and measure how you move — no sensors, no wearables.

WHAT YOU GET
• Automatic swing counting from video
• Swing range: how far your arm swings through each stroke
• Knee bend: how much you load your legs into each shot, compared with published research on tennis players
• A readiness score and its trend over time
• A replay of your strokes with a skeleton overlay, so you can review your technique

FOR COACHES AND TEAMS
Create a team, invite players with a code, set each player's routine, and follow their progress in one place. Players who train on their own keep their data private to themselves.

PRIVACY
Videos are recorded without audio and stored privately. No ads, no third-party analytics, and your data is never sold. Delete any session, or your whole account, from inside the app.

IMPORTANT
APeX provides training feedback based on computer-vision movement analysis — pose estimation, balance, and range of motion. It is not a medical device, is not a diagnostic tool, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a physician before beginning any exercise program.
