APP NAME
APeX: Athlete Performance

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Record a set on video. Get rep counts, balance and range-of-motion feedback.

FULL DESCRIPTION
APeX turns a short phone video into movement feedback. Prop your phone up, record a set, and APeX reconstructs your movement in 3D to count your reps and measure how you move — no wearables, no markers.

WHAT YOU GET
• Automatic rep counting from video
• Left/right balance, to spot one side doing more of the work
• Range of motion for every rep, against research-based targets
• A readiness score and its trend over time
• A replay of your set with a skeleton overlay, so you can review your form

SPORTS AND EXERCISES
General fitness (squat, vertical jump, pull-up, push-up, ab crunch), padel strokes, and tennis strokes and serves.

FOR COACHES AND TEAMS
Create a team, invite athletes with a code, set each athlete's routine, and follow their readiness and history in one place. Athletes who train on their own keep their data private to themselves.

PRIVACY
Videos are recorded without audio and stored privately. No ads, no third-party analytics, and your data is never sold. Delete any session, or your whole account, from inside the app.

IMPORTANT
APeX provides training feedback based on computer-vision movement analysis — pose estimation, balance, and range of motion. It is not a medical device, is not a diagnostic tool, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a physician before beginning any exercise program.
